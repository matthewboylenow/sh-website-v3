05 · Components

Buttons

Rust for primary CTAs (Give, Plan your visit), navy for secondary-primary, ghost for overlay surfaces. Every button is a pill.

Primary — Rust.btn .btn-rust
Use for the highest-intent action on a surface. Do not place two rust buttons adjacent — pair with a navy or ghost secondary instead.
Primary — Navy.btn .btn-primary
Secondary — Navy outline.btn .btn-secondary
Ghost — over imagery.btn .btn-ghost-light
Ghost buttons assume a dark or photographic background. Do not use on white.
Gold accent.btn .btn-gold
Reserved for seasonal / celebratory moments — Harvest Fest, Christmas banners, feast-day events.
