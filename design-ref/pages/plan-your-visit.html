In three steps

Your first Sunday

01

Pick a Mass

Sun 8 AM, 10 AM (family), 12 PM, 6 PM (youth-led). Saturday Vigil at 5. All Masses are welcome to everyone.

02

Come as you are

No dress code. Arrive 10 minutes early and our greeters will show you around. Restrooms, cry room, and family seating are all clearly marked.

03

Stay for coffee

After the 10 AM family Mass, join us in the parish hall for coffee and donuts. No agenda — just a chance to say hello.

Send us a note first

We'd love to meet you before Sunday.

Tell us a bit about yourself and we'll make sure a greeter is watching for you. We'll also follow up with anything you need — parking, kids' programs, sacraments.

What to expect

Mass is 55 minutes

Music, Scripture, homily, Communion. You don't need to know when to stand or sit — just follow along.

Free parking

Large lot behind the church plus overflow across the street. Accessible spots at the main entrance.

Kids are welcome

Cry room off the narthex. Kids Corner (ages 2–5) during the 10 AM Mass. No shushing required.
